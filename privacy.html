Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Short version: Weave has no backend, no account system, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Your workout data is read from Apple Health, processed entirely on your iPhone, and never transmitted anywhere by Weave. Personal data only comes up where other services are technically involved (Apple Maps, hosting this website, email). All of that is listed below, together with your rights.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Weave iOS app ("Weave"), published by Rinnebühl Labs, and to this website (workoutstories.app). It describes what data Weave accesses on your device and, more importantly, what it does not do with it, and what data comes up when you visit the website or contact us by email.

Data Weave reads from Apple Health

With your permission, Weave reads the following workout data from HealthKit to build your share cards:

  • Workout type (e.g. run, ride, walk)
  • Distance, duration, and pace
  • Active energy burned (calories)
  • Heart rate
  • Elevation gain
  • Route / GPS path recorded by your Apple Watch or iPhone (HKWorkoutRoute)

This access is read-only. Weave never writes to, modifies, or deletes anything in Apple Health. Permission is requested in stages: workout-list access when you first open the app, and route or heart-rate detail only when you open an individual workout.

Where this data goes

Your workout data stays on your device. Weave has no server, no cloud sync, and no database of any kind. All card rendering, route drawing, and workout merging happens locally on your iPhone using Apple's own frameworks (HealthKit, MapKit, Core Graphics). Rinnebühl Labs does not receive, see, or store any of your health or workout data at any point.

Apple Maps (MapKit)

Route cards with a map use Apple's MapKit. To draw them, your iPhone loads the map for your route's area directly from Apple Maps, as any map app does. Apple technically receives your IP address and the requested map area; your workout values (heart rate, pace, etc.) are not transmitted. The request goes straight from your device to Apple; Rinnebühl Labs receives nothing from it.

The provider is Apple (for users in the EU: Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (the map display you asked for) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in showing routes on a map). How Apple processes this data is described in Apple's Apple Maps privacy information and the Apple Privacy Policy.

Photo library access

When you export a card or sticker, Weave saves the resulting image to your photo library. This only happens when you explicitly tap export or save — Weave does not read your existing photos.

Analytics, advertising, and third parties

Weave does not integrate any analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or other third-party SDKs. There are no trackers of any kind in the app. The app does not share or sell any data about you or your usage — because it collects none in the first place.

When you download Weave from the App Store, Apple processes the data this requires (e.g. your Apple Account and the purchase) as an independent controller. If you have agreed in iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple gives us aggregated statistics and crash reports in App Store Connect that we cannot link to any person. You control this under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

In-app purchases

Weave Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. Rinnebühl Labs never sees your payment details — Apple handles billing and shares only the entitlement status (whether you own Weave Pro) with the app.

Location services

Weave does not request or use live device location (there is no location permission prompt). Route maps are built from the GPS path already recorded with your workout in Apple Health, not from tracking your current location.

Website: hosting and server logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the website, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, whether or not you are signed in to GitHub, together with technical details such as the time, the page requested, and your browser. Rinnebühl Labs has no access to these logs and does not analyze them. GitHub decides how long it keeps them.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in delivering the website securely and reliably. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; the transfer to the USA relies on the EU Commission's adequacy decision for it (Art. 45 GDPR). Details: GitHub's privacy statement.

The website loads no external fonts, scripts, or trackers, sets no cookies, and uses no analytics tools.

Website: language setting

When you switch the website's language, your browser stores that choice locally (localStorage, key weave-lang) so you see the right language on your next visit. The value never leaves your device. Storing it is strictly necessary for this feature you asked for (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time through your browser's website data.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR when it concerns the app or a purchase, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in answering requests). We delete your email once your request is resolved, unless statutory retention periods (e.g. tax and commercial law, up to 6 years for business correspondence) require us to keep it.

Our mailbox is operated on our behalf by Namecheap, Inc. (Private Email), 4600 East Washington Street, Suite 300, Phoenix, AZ 85034, USA. Your rights

Where we process personal data about you, the GDPR gives you the right to:

  • access the data stored about you (Art. 15)
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16)
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17)
  • restrict processing (Art. 18)
  • data portability (Art. 20)
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21)
  • withdraw consent at any time, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3))

Just email info@rinnebuehl.de. The most we hold about you is your emails to us and, if you signed up for the beta, your email address.

Your workout data lives only on your iPhone in Apple Health; you delete it yourself in the Health app, and deleting Weave removes all of the app's data from your device. For data that Apple (App Store, Apple Maps) or GitHub (server logs) process as independent controllers, you can exercise your rights with them directly: Apple at privacy.apple.com, GitHub through its privacy contact form.

Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state where you live or work. The authority responsible for Rinnebühl Labs is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg.

No automated decision-making

We make no automated decisions and do no profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

Children's privacy

Weave is not directed at children. The app does not collect data from anyone, of any age.
