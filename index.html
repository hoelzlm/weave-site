Features

Built for the moment right after a workout

Everything reads straight from Apple Health. Nothing leaves your device unless you tap share.

⌘

Merge multiple workouts into one card

Pick any 2+ workouts — a training block, a race weekend, a week of rides — and combine them into a single recap card with combined stats and a merged route. No other workout app lets you build a custom multi-workout recap on demand.

▦

Four card styles

Clean, Bold, Route First, and Recap — pick the look that matches your workout and your feed. Every template works for single or merged workouts.

✂

Cards or transparent stickers

Export a full card, or a transparent sticker for layering into your own Instagram Story design.

〰

Vector route rendering

Draw your route as clean line art instead of a map screenshot — faster to render, great for a minimal look.

🔒

On-device, always

No account, no linked social network, no backend server. Your workout data never leaves your phone. Read the privacy policy →
