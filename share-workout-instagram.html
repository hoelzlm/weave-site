How to share an Apple Watch workout on Instagram

Updated October 9, 2026

Your Apple Watch records the run, the ride or the lifting session, and your iPhone keeps it in the Fitness app and Apple Health. Instagram, though, posts images and videos, not workouts. Weave is an iPhone app that turns the workouts in Apple Health into Instagram-ready cards: route map, distance, time, pace and heart rate laid out in a template, sized for a Story or the feed. It reads your workouts on your iPhone, with no account and no Strava. Weave is free to download on the App Store.

Share a workout card to your Story or feed

  1. Open Weave and allow it to read your workouts from Apple Health.
  2. Tap a workout, then tap Create Card.
  3. Pick a template. Clean, Bold and Lift (made for strength sessions) are free; Route First and Recap come with Weave Pro.
  4. Choose a theme, which stats to show, and the format: 9:16 Story or 1:1 Square for the feed.
  5. Tap Export, then Share and choose Instagram in the share sheet. Or tap Save to Photos and pick the card from your camera roll in Instagram.

Put your stats on your own photo

Want your photo from the trail or the finish line in the background? Tap Share as Sticker instead. Weave exports the card’s route and stats as a PNG with a transparent background, made to be layered over your own photo or video. How the transparency comes through depends on the app you send it to.

Weave Pro

Free cards carry a small Weave watermark. Weave Pro is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription, that removes it and unlocks the Pro templates, vector routes and merging several workouts into one card. Route cards with a map load the map for your route’s area from Apple Maps; your workout data never goes to us.
