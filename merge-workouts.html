How to merge Apple Watch workouts

Updated October 9, 2026

Once a workout is saved, Fitness and Apple Health keep it on its own. A week of commutes, a training block, a race weekend or the two halves of a run you paused at a café all stay separate workouts. Weave, an iPhone app for workout cards, shows them together on one Recap card, an image to share. It only reads your workouts from Apple Health and never writes to it, so your workouts, rings and stats stay exactly as recorded.

Merge finished Apple Watch workouts into one card

  1. In Weave’s workout list, tap Merge at the top.
  2. Tap the workouts you want on the card. Once you’ve picked the first, only workouts of the same activity can be selected: runs with runs, rides with rides.
  3. Tap Merge 3 Workouts (the number follows your selection).
  4. Weave opens the card in the Recap template: total distance and time, average pace (or speed, for rides), a row for each workout and the routes drawn together. Up to five rows fit; more workouts show as “+ N more workouts”. Turn on Color Each Route to give every route its own color.
  5. Tap Export, then Share or Save to Photos. Share as Sticker gives you a version with a transparent background.

Merging is part of Weave Pro, a one-time in-app purchase of $6.99, not a subscription. Pro also unlocks the Route First and Recap templates and vector routes, and removes the watermark. Weave itself is free to download on the App Store.

Good to know

  • Apple Health and Fitness stay untouched. Your workouts stay separate there; the merge exists only on your card.
  • One activity per card. Runs go with runs, rides with rides, so the combined pace and distance mean something.
  • No account, no Strava. The card is made on your iPhone, and your workout data never goes to us.
